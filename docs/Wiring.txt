# Wiring - BMW Z3 cluster bench setup
Wire with USB unplugged and PSU output OFF.

## Rails
- Left  (-) = ESP32 GND   (only ESP32 things here)
- Right (+) = PSU +12 V
- Right (-) = PSU GND
- Left (-) and right (-) must NEVER be connected.

## Cluster power
- X16 pin 22  -> right (+)
- X16 pin 23  -> right (+)
- X17 pin 4   -> right (+)
- X17 pin 16  -> right (+)
- X16 pin 24  -> right (-)
- X17 pin 3   -> right (-)
- X16 pin 13 <-> X16 pin 25 (bridge)

## 4N35 pinout (dot = pin 1, top left)
  pin 1 | o     | pin 6
  pin 2 |       | pin 5
  pin 3 |       | pin 4
  ESP32 side    12 V side

## 4N35 #1 - Tachometer
- pin 1 -> 220 ohm -> GPIO 18
- pin 2 -> left (-)  ESP32 GND
- pin 3 -> nothing
- pin 4 -> right (-) PSU GND
- pin 5 -> X16 pin 20  AND  1 kohm -> right (+)
- pin 6 -> nothing

## 4N35 #2 - Speedometer
- pin 1 -> 220 ohm -> GPIO 19
- pin 2 -> left (-)  ESP32 GND
- pin 3 -> nothing
- pin 4 -> right (-) PSU GND
- pin 5 -> X271 pin 4   (no pull-up, the cluster has one inside)
- pin 6 -> nothing

## IRF9540 pinout (P-MOSFET, text facing you, legs down)
  G  D  S
  1  2  3
- G = Gate   (on/off control)
- D = Drain  (out to the light)
- S = Source (from +12 V)
- The metal tab is connected to D - keep it away from other parts.
- Each leg in its own breadboard row.

## Light switched to +12 V (4N35 + IRF9540)
How it works: GPIO HIGH -> 4N35 on -> gate pulled to PSU GND -> MOSFET on
-> +12 V to the cluster pin -> light on. The 10 kohm keeps the gate at
+12 V (MOSFET off) when the 4N35 is off.

  (+) rail ----+-----------+
               |           |
             10 kohm      wire
               |           |
  4N35 pin 5 - G     D     S
                     |
                cluster pin

## 4N35 #3 + IRF9540 #1 - Left turn signal
- 4N35 pin 1 -> 220 ohm -> GPIO 16
- 4N35 pin 2 -> left (-)  ESP32 GND
- 4N35 pin 4 -> right (-) PSU GND
- 4N35 pin 5 -> MOSFET G
- MOSFET G   -> 10 kohm -> right (+)
- MOSFET S   -> right (+)
- MOSFET D   -> X16 pin 4

## 4N35 #4 + IRF9540 #2 - Right turn signal
- 4N35 pin 1 -> 220 ohm -> GPIO 17
- 4N35 pin 2 -> left (-)  ESP32 GND
- 4N35 pin 4 -> right (-) PSU GND
- 4N35 pin 5 -> MOSFET G
- MOSFET G   -> 10 kohm -> right (+)
- MOSFET S   -> right (+)
- MOSFET D   -> X16 pin 3

## 4N35 #7 + IRF9540 #3 - High beam
- 4N35 pin 1 -> 220 ohm -> GPIO 4   (the LOLIN32 Lite has no GPIO 21/23)
- 4N35 pin 2 -> left (-)  ESP32 GND
- 4N35 pin 4 -> right (-) PSU GND
- 4N35 pin 5 -> MOSFET G
- MOSFET G   -> 10 kohm -> right (+)
- MOSFET S   -> right (+)
- MOSFET D   -> X16 pin 1

## 4N35 #8 + IRF9540 #4 - Fog lights
- 4N35 pin 1 -> 220 ohm -> GPIO 13
- 4N35 pin 2 -> left (-)  ESP32 GND
- 4N35 pin 4 -> right (-) PSU GND
- 4N35 pin 5 -> MOSFET G
- MOSFET G   -> 10 kohm -> right (+)
- MOSFET S   -> right (+)
- MOSFET D   -> X17 pin 8

## ULN2803A pinout (notch at the top, pin 1 = top left)
  IN 1  | 1   18 | OUT 1
  IN 2  | 2   17 | OUT 2
  ...   |  ...   | ...
  IN 8  | 8   11 | OUT 8
  GND   | 9   10 | COM
- IN 1 (pin 1) controls OUT 1 (pin 18), IN 2 controls OUT 2 (pin 17), etc.
- pin 9  -> right (-) PSU GND
- pin 10 -> right (+) +12 V

## Light switched to ground (4N35 + ULN2803A channel)
How it works: GPIO HIGH -> 4N35 on -> +12 V to the ULN input -> ULN pulls
the cluster pin to ground -> light on. The 10 kohm keeps the ULN input at
ground (light off) when the 4N35 is off.
NOTE: the 4N35 is flipped compared to the MOSFET lights:
pin 5 -> +12 V, pin 4 -> ULN input.

## 4N35 #5 + ULN channel 1 - Park brake (P)
- 4N35 pin 1 -> 220 ohm -> GPIO 25
- 4N35 pin 2 -> left (-)  ESP32 GND
- 4N35 pin 5 -> right (+) +12 V
- 4N35 pin 4 -> ULN pin 1 (IN 1)
- ULN pin 1  -> 10 kohm -> right (-)
- ULN pin 18 (OUT 1) -> X16 pin 17

## 4N35 #6 + ULN channel 2 - ASC
- 4N35 pin 1 -> 220 ohm -> GPIO 27
- 4N35 pin 2 -> left (-)  ESP32 GND
- 4N35 pin 5 -> right (+) +12 V
- 4N35 pin 4 -> ULN pin 2 (IN 2)
- ULN pin 2  -> 10 kohm -> right (-)
- ULN pin 17 (OUT 2) -> X17 pin 12

## Continuity check before power-up
- left (-)  <-> right (-)   : NO beep
- left (-)  <-> right (+)   : NO beep
- right (+) <-> right (-)   : NO beep
- X16 pin 24 / X17 pin 3 <-> right (-) : beep
- 4N35 #1-#4, #7, #8 pin 4 <-> right (-) : beep (#5, #6 pin 4 go to the ULN instead)
- each MOSFET D <-> right (+)          : NO beep (if it beeps, D and S are swapped)
- ULN pin 9 <-> right (-)              : beep

## Power-up order
1. PSU on (12 V, 1 A) without USB -> current > 0 A, odometer on
2. Plug in USB -> current must NOT change
3. Test: ',' = left, '.' = right, 'h' = hazards (blink with the blue LED),
   'p' = park brake (starts ON), 'a' = ASC on/off,
   'b' = high beam, 'f' = fog

## Troubleshooting a light that won't turn on (light ON in the code)
Black probe on the right ground for each side!
- ESP GND -> GPIO pin        : ~3.3 V  (else: code/upload/wrong pin)
- ESP GND -> 4N35 pin 1      : ~1.2 V  (3.3 V = pin 2 not on ESP GND, 0 V = 220 ohm/wire missing)
- PSU GND -> 4N35 pin 5      : ~0.1 V  (12 V = 4N35 not switching)
- PSU GND -> MOSFET G (leg)  : ~0.1 V  (12 V while pin 5 is low = broken wire / leg not seated)
- PSU GND -> MOSFET D        : ~12 V   (0 V = G/D/S wrong or S not on +12 V)
